# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text for Golden Star Company, adhere to this brand persona:

## Brand Personality Keywords
* Institutional & Trustworthy
* Direct, Precise, & Efficient
* Global Logistics Authority
* Commercial & Professional

## Grammar & Mechanics Rules
* **Headings:** Use Title Case for main headings (H1, H2). Use sentence case for subheadings (H3+).
* **Punctuation:** Avoid exclamation points (!) in standard B2B trade copy. Use periods for complete sentences.
* **Clarity:** Prefer active voice over passive voice. Emphasize contract fidelity, verifiable inspection, and seamless shipping execution.
